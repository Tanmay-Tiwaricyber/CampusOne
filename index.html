01 — the problem

campus life runs on scattered systems.

Portals, notice boards, spreadsheets and paper forms — each holding a piece of the truth, none showing the whole picture.

Pain 01Information is spread across portals, notices and paper forms
Pain 02Requests have no visible status or owner
Pain 03Routine tasks still need office visits

02 — solution overview

one platform. four roles.

Everyone sees only what they should — in a layout that feels familiar from the first login.

student.

Track documents, fees, marks and requests — all in one place.

role / student

parent.

Attendance and fee alerts with limited, verified access.

role / parent

teacher.

Attendance, assignments and marks for assigned classes only.

role / teacher

admin.

Approvals, reports and complete audit trails.

role / admin

Plus configurable roles: HOD, Registrar, Accountant, Librarian and more.

The complete picture

one campus. every workflow.

The complete intended product scope from our PRD. Features are planned in phases; availability depends on the release and institutional configuration.

01 / People & access

Authentication & accounts

Secure sign-in, recovery, optional multi-factor authentication, onboarding, sessions and configurable roles and permissions.

Student information

A student 360 profile with enrollment, academic history, contacts, progression, achievements, records and digital ID.

Parents & guardians

Verified child linking, attendance and performance views, fee information, communication and meeting requests.

Admissions & enrollment

Applications, eligibility, document checks, merit lists, seat allocation, admission decisions and student onboarding.

02 / Academics & learning

Academic structure

Campuses, departments, programmes, subjects, curriculum, credits, sessions, registration and academic calendars.

Timetable & scheduling

Class, teacher, room and exam schedules, substitutions, conflict checks and change notifications.

Attendance & leave

Lecture and subject attendance, shortage alerts, correction history, leave applications and approvals.

Exams & results

Exam scheduling, assessments, marks, configurable grading, approved result publication, transcripts and re-evaluation.

Assignments & learning

Study materials, assignment submissions, teacher feedback, quizzes, question banks and learning progress.

03 / Student services & finance

Document vault

Upload, preview, download, verify and resubmit documents with reviewer remarks, status and history.

Applications & certificates

Track leave, certificates and other requests through configurable approval workflows, remarks and notifications.

Fees, payments & finance

Fee structures, ledgers, payment gateway integration, receipts, scholarships, refunds and reconciliation.

No-dues & clearance

Department-wise clearances, pending dues, approvals, audit history and downloadable no-dues certificates.

Library & digital library

Book catalogue, lending, reservations, renewals, overdue fines, e-books and borrowing history.

04 / Campus life & communication

Notices & communication

Targeted notices, in-app alerts, configured email, SMS and push, messaging, polls and parent-teacher meetings.

Grievances & welfare

Track complaints and confidential cases with owners, escalation, responses and policy-controlled access.

Events, clubs & sports

Event calendars, registration, clubs, competitions, participation certificates and achievements.

Placement & careers

Job and internship listings, eligibility, resumes, applications, interviews, offers and alumni mentorship.

Hostel management

Room and bed allocation, hostel requests, fees, leave passes, complaints, mess feedback and move-out.

Transport management

Routes, stops, registrations, vehicle records, schedules, fees, maintenance and transport complaints.

05 / Institutional operations

Faculty & human resources

Staff profiles, teaching workload, attendance, leave, recruitment, payroll and restricted employee records.

Research & innovation

Student projects, teams, milestones, publications, patents, incubation and research records.

Facilities & assets

Campus spaces, equipment inventory, bookings, maintenance requests, work orders and servicing history.

Visitors & front desk

Visitor registration, appointments, check-in and check-out, passes, enquiries and visitor history.

Compliance & governance

Committees, meeting minutes, policies, accreditation evidence, audits, retention and compliance tasks.

06 / Insights & productivity

Dashboards, reports & analytics

Role-specific dashboards, operational reports and permission-controlled PDF or spreadsheet exports.

Calendar & personal utilities

Academic and personal calendars, deadlines, to-do lists, reminders, directories, feedback and polls.

Optional intelligence

Early-warning analytics, OCR assistance, search and productivity suggestions; core workflows do not depend on AI.

04 — how it works

from request to resolved.

Four steps. One visible thread. No chasing counters.

01

submit.

Student uploads a document or raises a request in under a minute.

02

review.

The right office verifies it — with remarks the student can see.

03

decide.

Approve, reject or ask for a resubmit. Every decision is logged.

04

track.

Status, owner and history stay visible until it is resolved.

    06 — security & trust

    designed for trust.

    Security and privacy are core principles of the proposed design. These safeguards would need to be implemented and tested in a real system.

    access

    01 / Access

    role-based access.

    Students, parents, teachers and admins each see only their own slice.

    storage

    02 / Storage

    private document storage.

    Use private storage and signed, expiring links rather than public document URLs.

    audit

    03 / Audit

    audit logs for every action.

    Who viewed, approved or changed what — timestamped and searchable.

    server

    04 / Server

    server-side permission checks.

    Enforce authorization on the server, not just in the interface.

    backup

    05 / Recovery

    backups and recovery.

    Plan automated backups and tested restore paths for documents and records.

    privacy

    06 / Privacy

    privacy by design.

    Minimum data collected, parents get limited verified access, nothing sold.

    08 — design thinking

    designed with people, not for them.

    Our concept follows a human-centred process: understand the problem, imagine possibilities and refine the idea.

    empathize.

    Consider the needs of students, parents and campus staff.

    define.

    The problem was visibility, not information.

    ideate.

    One thread per request, with an owner and a status.
